The Big Picture 宏观背景

The Problem

AI in group chats is often awkward and socially unaware.

Current RAG retrieves Facts, but misses Social Context.

The Solution: Social-RAG

Retrieving Group Interactions (chats, reactions) to ground AI generation.

  • Understands "Who knows what"
  • Learns from implicit feedback
  • Acts like an "Invited Guest"

The Gap 研究背景与差异

Traditional RAG

  • Source: Wikipedia, Docs
  • Focus: Factual Accuracy
  • Output: "Here is the answer."
  • Socially Blind
VS

Social-RAG

  • Source: Chat History, Reactions
  • Focus: Social Relevance
  • Output: "@Alice, since you liked..."
  • Socially Grounded

Methodology 研究方法论

Formative Study

Interviews (N=13) & Survey (N=26)

Insight

Users want neutral, short summaries with social context.

Takeaways 总结与启示

Context is King

Social context is as important as factual context for group AI.

Implicit > Explicit

Learning from digital traces is better than asking users to fill forms.

Social Actor

AI should behave like a polite "Invited Guest", not a spammer.
